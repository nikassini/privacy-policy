Privacy Policy

German for Work
Last updated: May 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to German for Work, developed by Christian Paquet. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data stored locally on your device

The App stores the following data exclusively on your device:

  • Your learning progress (flashcard repetitions, review dates, correct/incorrect answers)
  • App settings (selected language, dark mode, daily goals, audio preferences, notification settings)
  • Learning statistics (daily reviews, streaks, minutes learned)
  • Custom vocabulary decks and cards you create

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any personal identification
  • Location data
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Device contacts
  • Analytics or usage tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers

3. Audio / Text-to-Speech

The App uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) system to pronounce German vocabulary. This is processed entirely on your device. We do not collect or process any audio data.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking services. If future versions introduce such services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Data Storage and Security

All learning data and settings are stored locally on your device. We have no access to this data. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right of access to your data
  • Right to erasure – delete all data by uninstalling the App
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
