Privacy Policy

German for Work
Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to German for Work (the "App"), developed by Christian Paquet (Nikassini). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

You can use the App without an account. Creating an account is optional and enables cloud sync of your learning data across your devices.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data stored locally on your device

The App stores the following data on your device:

  • Your learning progress (flashcard repetitions, review dates, correct/incorrect answers)
  • App settings (selected language, dark mode, daily goals, audio preferences, notification settings)
  • Learning statistics (daily reviews, streaks, minutes learned)
  • Custom vocabulary decks and cards you create

If you do not create an account, this data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. If you create an account, it is synchronized with the cloud as described in section 2.2.

2.2 Data we process if you create an account (optional)

If you create an account or sign in, we process:

  • Account data: your email address and a unique user ID. You can sign in with email and password or with Google Sign-In (via Firebase Authentication). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password. When you use Google Sign-In, Firebase receives basic profile information from Google (such as your email address, name and profile picture link). The App does not use your name or picture.
  • Synchronized app data: the data listed in section 2.1 (learning progress, settings, statistics, and the custom decks and cards you create), together with the time of the last change. This data is stored as one document per user in Cloud Firestore.

2.3 Technical data

When the App connects to Google's servers (Firebase), Google processes technical connection data, such as your IP address, as needed to provide the service.

2.4 Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect:

  • Location data
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Device contacts
  • Analytics or usage tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers

If you do not create an account, we also do not collect your name, email address or any other personal identification.

3. Audio / Text-to-Speech

The App uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) system to pronounce German vocabulary. The text to be spoken is handled by the speech engine installed on your device. We do not receive, collect or store any text or audio data from this feature.

4. Third-Party Services

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and Google Sign-In are used only if you create an account or sign in. Provider: Google Ireland Limited (EEA) and Google LLC (USA). Our Cloud Firestore database is located in Frankfurt, Germany (europe-west3). Google may process data in other countries, including the United States; such transfers are covered by Google's data processing terms, which include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (and, where applicable, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

More information: Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

Reminders: daily learning reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service is used.

The App does not use third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking services. If future versions introduce additional services (for example for subscription management), this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Purposes and Legal Bases (GDPR)

  • Providing your account and cloud sync: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the service you request).
  • Keeping the service secure and preventing abuse (technical data): Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).

Data that stays on your device is not processed by us.

6. Data Storage, Security and Retention

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete the App's data or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App deletes the locally stored data, but it does not delete an account or cloud data.
  • Account data and synchronized data are kept until you delete your account.
  • Deletion: in the App, go to Settings → Cloud → "Delete account and data". This immediately deletes your account and your cloud data and resets the App's data on that device. Backup copies at Google may remain for a limited time in line with Google's own retention policies. You can also request deletion by email; see how to delete your account.
  • Security: data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS), Google encrypts Cloud Firestore data at rest, and access rules ensure that each user can only access their own data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right of access to your data
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure – delete your account and cloud data in the App at any time, or request deletion by email
  • Right to restriction of processing and to object
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

For requests that you cannot handle in the App, contact us at the address below.
