Delete your account and data

German for Work
Developer: Christian Paquet (Nikassini)
Last updated: October 20, 2026

Option 1: Delete your account in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the German for Work app and go to Settings.
  2. In the Cloud section, tap "Delete account and data".
  3. Read the warning and confirm.

Your account and all of your cloud data are deleted immediately and permanently. The app's learning data on that device is reset as well. This cannot be undone.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to avoid further charges.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer use the app, send an email to nikassini@web.de with the subject "Delete my German for Work account". Please write from the email address you used for your account and tell us whether you signed in with email and password or with Google. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account data (email address and user ID).
  • All data synced to the cloud: your decks and custom cards, learning progress, statistics and app settings.

What may be kept

  • Backup copies at Google (Firebase) may remain for a limited time in line with Google's own retention policies.
  • If you purchase a paid subscription, records of the transaction are kept by Google Play and by us as long as the law requires, for example for tax and accounting purposes.

Using the app without an account

If you never created an account, we do not hold any data about you. Your learning data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

See also our Privacy Policy.
